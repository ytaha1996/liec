# 1. Getting Started

## Signing in

You sign in with an **email address and password** given to you by an administrator. There is no
public sign-up — accounts are created for you.

- If you type the wrong details, the system says "Invalid email or password". It never reveals
  whether the email exists.
- After several failed attempts in a row your account is **temporarily locked** to stop anyone
  guessing passwords. Wait a few minutes, or ask an administrator.
- If you try to open a page while signed out, you are sent to the sign-in screen — and once you
  sign in you land on **the page you originally wanted**, not the home page.
- Your session lasts **8 hours**. After that you are asked to sign in again. If your session runs
  out while you are working, the system notices and returns you to sign-in rather than showing
  errors.

## Finding your way around

The system is divided into four **apps**, shown as coloured areas:

| App | What lives there |
|---|---|
| **Operations** (teal) | Dashboard, Shipments, Reports — the daily work |
| **Master Data** (dark blue) | Customers, Warehouses, Good Types, Pricing, Suppliers, Supply Orders, Currencies |
| **Communications** (purple) | Messaging Logs, Group Export |
| **Admin** (red) | Users |

Three ways to move around:

1. **The top bar** shows the pages of the app you are currently in, so it stays uncluttered.
2. **The app launcher** — the grid icon at the top left — opens a panel showing every app and page
   you have access to. It has a search box, and on a phone it fills the screen.
3. **Quick search** — press **Ctrl+K** (or **⌘K** on a Mac), or click the magnifying glass. Start
   typing and it finds pages, shipments, packages and customers, then jumps you straight there.

**You only ever see what your role allows.** Pages you cannot use do not appear in the menus at
all, and typing their address directly sends you back to the Dashboard. See
[Users, Roles & Security](11-users-and-security.md).

## The Dashboard

Your landing page after signing in. It shows:

- **Counts** of Customers, Shipments and Packages
- **Shipments by Status** and **Packages by Status** — how much work sits at each stage
- **Pending Charges** — the total value of goods in the system that have not yet been handed over.
  *Warehouse staff (the Field role) do not see this figure.*
- Alerts for containers needing attention, and a shortcut to the shipments list

## Working on a phone or tablet

Every screen works on a phone. Menus collapse into a hamburger button, tables scroll sideways
inside their own frame (the page itself never scrolls sideways), and pop-up forms fill the screen
so they are comfortable to fill in with a thumb.

## Your profile

Click your initial in the top-right corner and choose your profile to:

- see your **username, email and role**
- **change your password** — you must enter your current password first

## Small things that make life easier

- **Tables** can be searched, sorted by clicking a column heading, filtered, and paged through.
  You can change how many rows show at once.
- **Confirmation prompts** appear before anything irreversible. Destructive actions are red.
- **Messages** appear briefly in the corner to confirm a save or explain a failure. When something
  is refused, the message says *why*, not just "error".
- **Dates** appear in a consistent format throughout, and long lists always tell you the total.
