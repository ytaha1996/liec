# LIEC Shipping Platform — What the System Does

A plain-English guide to every feature in the system. No technical knowledge needed.

## What this system is for

LIEC moves customers' goods between warehouses in **Lebanon, Gabon, China and Dubai**. Many
customers' packages travel together inside one shared shipping container. The system keeps track
of all of it: who sent what, how much it weighs and measures, what they should be charged, where
it is right now, and what paperwork has been produced.

It replaces the spreadsheets and paper order forms the business used before, and it is used only
by company staff — customers do not log in.

## The five things worth knowing up front

1. **A shipment is a container.** Packages belong to a shipment. Almost everything you do starts
   by opening a shipment.
2. **Everything moves in one direction.** A package goes Draft → Received → Packed → and onward.
   The system will not let you skip a step or go backwards.
3. **Photos are compulsory at three points.** No departure photo means the container cannot leave;
   no arrival photo means the goods cannot be handed over.
4. **Weight is shown in tons, volume in CBM (cubic metres).** You *type* weight in kilograms; the
   screens display tons.
5. **Nothing is silently deleted.** Cancelled items stay visible, and every important change is
   recorded with who did it and when.

## A note on money

The system records what a customer **was charged**. It does not record payments received, so
wherever you see "Total Billed" it means "what we invoiced", not "what has been collected".
